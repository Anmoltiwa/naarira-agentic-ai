NAARIRA — RETURN & REFUND POLICY
Canonical Knowledge Document
Last verified: 15 September 2026
Source: https://naarira.com/pages/refund-policy

Purpose
This document is the canonical source for return, refund, exchange, damaged-item, and cancellation questions in the Naarira AI assistant.

RETURN WINDOW
Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving their order.

RETURN ELIGIBILITY
Returned items must:
- Be unused and unworn.
- Have the original tags attached.
- Be in the original packaging.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
The following are not eligible for return:
- Customized products.
- Altered garments.
- Gift cards.
- Clearance or final-sale items.

REFUND PROCESS
Refunds are initiated after the returned product passes quality inspection at Naarira's warehouse.
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

PREPAID ORDERS
For prepaid orders, the refund amount is credited to the original payment method.

COD ORDERS
For COD orders, the refund is processed through a Cashfree payment link.

EXCHANGE
Naarira does not offer exchanges on its products.

DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR INCORRECT ITEMS
Customers should contact Naarira within 24 hours of delivery and provide photos plus an unboxing video.

CANCELLATION
Orders may be cancelled before dispatch.
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation requests cannot be accepted.
