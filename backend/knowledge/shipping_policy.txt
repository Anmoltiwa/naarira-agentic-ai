NAARIRA — SHIPPING POLICY
Canonical Knowledge Document
Last verified: 15 September 2026
Source: https://naarira.com/pages/shipping-policy

Purpose
This document is the canonical source for shipping and delivery questions in the Naarira AI assistant.

PROCESSING TIME
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

DELIVERY TIMELINE
Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days across India.

SHIPPING CHARGES
Free shipping is available on orders above ₹999.

ORDER TRACKING
Once an order is dispatched, tracking details are shared with the registered email ID and contact number.

DELIVERY DELAYS
Delays may occur during festivals or because of weather or other unforeseen circumstances. Naarira will keep customers informed.

SHIPPING ADDRESS CHANGES
Customers can update their shipping address before the order is dispatched. They should contact Naarira support immediately if an address change is required.

HOW TO TRACK
After dispatch, a tracking link is provided through email and SMS so the shipment can be tracked.

SUPPORT
Email: care@naarira.com
Business hours: Monday–Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM IST
