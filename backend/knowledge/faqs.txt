Purpose
This document contains concise customer-facing FAQ answers for the Naarira AI assistant.
For policy-sensitive questions, the dedicated Shipping Policy and Return & Refund Policy are authoritative.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and standard delivery takes 5–7 business days.

Q: Is shipping free?
A: Free shipping is available on orders above ₹999.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Once the order is dispatched, tracking details are shared through the registered email ID and contact number. A tracking link is also provided via email and SMS.

Q: Can I change my shipping address?
A: Yes. A shipping address can be updated before the order is dispatched. Contact Naarira support immediately to request a change.

Q: What is Naarira's return policy?
A: Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving the order, subject to the return conditions.

Q: What are the return conditions?
A: The item must be unused and unworn, with original tags attached, and in original packaging.

Q: Which items cannot be returned?
A: Customized products, altered garments, gift cards, and clearance or final-sale items are non-returnable.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the return is received and passes quality inspection.

Q: Where is my prepaid refund credited?
A: Prepaid refunds are credited to the original payment method.

Q: How are COD refunds processed?
A: COD refunds are processed through a Cashfree payment link.

Q: Do you offer exchanges?
A: No. Naarira's dedicated Return & Refund Policy states that exchanges are not offered.

Q: I received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item. What should I do?
A: Contact Naarira within 24 hours of delivery and provide photos plus an unboxing video.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, but only before dispatch. Once the order has been shipped, cancellation cannot be accepted.

Q: Where can I get help?
A: Email care@naarira.com. Support hours are Monday–Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM IST.
